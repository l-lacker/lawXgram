# Tools

- These are manual browser-based asset helpers, not part of the normal Gradle build.
- `BinaryObjConverter.html` and `EmojiTextureMaker.html` are for targeted asset generation; avoid mass regeneration unless explicitly asked.
- Keep outputs compatible with the existing asset locations and naming under `TMessagesProj/src/main/assets`.
- Do not mix one-off local experiments into these files.
